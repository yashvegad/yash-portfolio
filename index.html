About

Clean code, fast sites, happy clients.

I'm Yash Vegad, a Front-End and WordPress developer based in Rajkot, Gujarat. For more than six years I've turned designs into responsive, SEO-friendly, high-performance websites for businesses that depend on their site to bring in work.

I write semantic HTML, modern CSS and ES6+ JavaScript, and I build custom WordPress themes with PHP, ACF and WooCommerce when a page builder isn't enough. When speed matters I optimize images, scripts and Core Web Vitals so pages load quickly on every device.

I work closely with designers and marketers, from Figma handoff to Google Analytics and Tag Manager setup, so what ships is clean, measurable and easy to maintain.
